# Polski (plPL)

- Address the player as *ty*, in lowercase within sentences. Keep the
  grammatical subject implicit when it is clear. Refer to other characters
  by name or by what they did.
- Use past tense for events in the diary. Use short labels in tables and
  concise commands on buttons. Use gendered forms where needed and the
  character is known. Summary lines should also read well without that detail.
- Use Polish quotation marks („…”), a decimal comma, day–month–year
  dates, 24-hour times, and the integer plural forms *one*, *few*, *many*
  (for example *1 zadanie*, *2 zadania*, *5 zadań*).
- Use *dziennik*, *wpis*, *notatka*, *obserwacja*, *powiadomienie* and
  *trasa*. Use *poziom* (or *poz.* in tight columns), *zadanie*, *łup*,
  *loch*, *rajd*, *pojedynek* and *pole bitwy* for game terms.
- Keep item, character, creature, and place names as provided by the client,
  including names in samples. Use the original name in a sentence without
  changing its spelling or adding an inflected ending.
- Prefer short labels on the match tickets, notification cards, controller
  bindings, and settings controls. Check Polish diacritics and wrapping in
  game, especially in handwritten captions.
