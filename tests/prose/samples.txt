=== week.lua (plain) ===
--- Monday 19 October ---
I worked my way from Ashenvale to Stonetalon Mountains. Our longest gap yet ended tonight: nearly three weeks after we last crossed paths, Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, was back. We kept a group going for most of the evening. For the second time, he stepped in when I needed it. In a one-sided trade, five [Lesser Healing Potion] came my way from him. He is catching me up, a level now, down from three when we last met.

The same night elf hunter again tonight. That's three days out of the last seven. And yet we've never had anything to do with each other.

Morthis probably killed me in Stonetalon Mountains. With at least five levels between us, it was never going to be a fair fight. In the end, I logged off at level 33.

--- Tuesday 20 October ---
I spent tonight in Ashenvale. Levels came too: 33 to 34. Second day in a row, and Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, was around again in Ashenvale. Brennick reached 33, and I levelled within minutes of him.

Kaelith and I grouped up in Ashenvale tonight. We'd never met before. A run through Blackfathom Deeps, six of seven bosses down. He died the most, three times. Not that it's a competition. I came away from it with a [Gravestone Scepter]. My 84 wasn't enough against Brennick's 87, so a [Strike of the Hydra] went to him. Kaelith went into the diary.

That was enough for one session.

--- Wednesday 21 October ---
No familiar faces this afternoon, in Ashenvale or anywhere else.

--- Thursday 22 October ---
In two and a half hours of play, I worked my way from Ashenvale to The Barrens. Rematch after rematch, and I finished three to one up on Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge.

Ilyra shared my fire. Before that, she and I exchanged five whispers.

Kaelith and I were grouped, and we went down within four seconds of each other. My fight with a Kolkar Stormer ended badly tonight. A gnome warlock was there for it. I ended the evening at level 35.

--- Saturday 24 October ---
I spent over three hours tonight between Stonetalon Mountains and Ashenvale. Level 36 by the end. I passed thirty-odd players along the way, the other faction among them.

Helped Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, kill Morthis. It was a long session: Brennick and I were in a group of three for most of the night. The night's highlight was Foreman Rigger, which we found together.

For the record, Ilyra, guildless when we last met, was in Wardens of the Moon, which was news to me. At one point, she whispered to me, "come find us at Astranaar sometime". Logged off in the small hours.

--- Saturday 7 November ---
Two and a half hours in Desolace. Level 41 now.

No meeting with Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, since Stonetalon Mountains, two weeks ago. I did hear from him three days ago. And that was the evening.

=== week.lua (warm) ===

He is catching me up, and we're still near enough to quest together.

The night elf hunter was near me at Astranaar again tonight, always in that same spot. Three days now. Strangely, I haven't introduced myself yet.

Morthis probably killed me again tonight. I'm almost fond of the routine. In the end, I logged off at level 33.

--- Tuesday 20 October ---
I spent tonight in Ashenvale. Level 34 now. It's coming along. As has become the habit, Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, and I crossed paths for the second day running. He hit 33 and I levelled right after. Good to do it together.

Kaelith and I got acquainted, over two quests that went by quickly. An hour and a half in Blackfathom Deeps, which was good fun. He went down three times; we all had our moments. I came away from it with a [Gravestone Scepter]. The roll went against me, so the [Strike of the Hydra] wasn't mine. He has a page in the diary now with the tag "friend".

That was where I stopped.

--- Wednesday 21 October ---
No levels this afternoon; I'm still 34.

--- Thursday 22 October ---
After logging on tonight, I set out from Ashenvale. When it was all over, I beat Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, fair and square, three to one, which I'll take with a smile.

Ilyra sat by my fire, which I liked. Before that, she and I whispered back and forth, which brightened my evening.

We fought a Kolkar Stormer together and fell together, Kaelith and I. It was some comfort that a gnome warlock was near me when I went down. That was the evening, and a good one.

--- Saturday 24 October ---
I logged on in Stonetalon Mountains tonight. By the end I was in Ashenvale. I started at 35 and finished at 36. On a busy night, I saw nearly twenty players in Stonetalon Mountains alone.

Helped Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, kill Morthis. Quest after quest, Brennick and I grouped for most of the night. Foreman Rigger crossed paths with us, and I was glad of the company for it.

For the record, there was a guild under Ilyra's name tonight, Wardens of the Moon, whatever the reason, and that's her business. I wrote this down: she whispered to me, "come find us at Astranaar sometime". After a long session, I didn't log off until after midnight, and no regrets.

--- Saturday 7 November ---
Settling in for the evening, I kept to Desolace, which suited me fine. Along the way, I picked up a level, from 40 to 41.

Brennick, my levelling buddy from Redridge, has a place in this diary, and it's been empty for two weeks. And that was the evening.
